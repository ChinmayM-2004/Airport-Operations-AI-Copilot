## 1. Purpose

This policy defines pricing and surge management requirements for rideshare services operating at LAX.

## 2. Standard Pricing

Operations teams may monitor pricing and demand conditions without additional approval.

Standard pricing must follow the approved platform pricing configuration.

## 3. Surge Pricing

Surge pricing may be activated when passenger demand significantly exceeds available driver supply.

The maximum surge multiplier permitted at LAX without additional approval is **1.8x**.

## 4. Surge Above 1.8x

Any proposed surge increase above **1.8x** requires approval from the **Airport Operations Manager** before activation.

The approval request must document:

* Current passenger demand
* Available driver supply
* Average passenger wait time
* Current cancellation rate
* Reason for the proposed increase
* Expected duration

## 5. Operational Disruptions

During major airport disruptions, operations teams may propose temporary pricing adjustments.

Examples include:

* Terminal closures
* Significant flight arrival surges
* Road closures
* Major traffic incidents
* Temporary staging restrictions

Any surge above 1.8x remains subject to the required approval unless a separately approved emergency procedure explicitly states otherwise.

## 6. Monitoring

During elevated surge conditions, operations teams should review:

* Passenger demand
* Driver supply
* Queue length
* Passenger wait time
* Cancellation rate

Pricing conditions should be reassessed at least every 30 minutes during an active disruption.

## 7. Reduction of Surge

When supply and demand return to normal operating conditions, surge should be reduced according to the approved operating plan.

## 8. Compliance

Surge above 1.8x must not be activated without the required approval.
