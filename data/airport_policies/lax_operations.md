# LAX Airport Operations Policy

**Policy ID:** LAX-OPS-001
**Airport:** Los Angeles International Airport (LAX)
**Policy Type:** Airport Operations
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

> **Synthetic Training Policy:** This document is fictional and created exclusively for the Airport Operations AI Copilot project.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines operational requirements for rideshare pickup, drop-off, staging, and driver movement at Los Angeles International Airport (LAX).

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to rideshare drivers and airport operations teams managing rideshare activity at LAX.

## 3. Passenger Pickup

1. Rideshare pickups must occur only in the designated rideshare pickup zones.
2. Drivers must not pick up passengers from unauthorized terminal curb locations.
3. Drivers must proceed to the pickup area only after receiving a valid passenger dispatch.
4. Drivers must follow airport traffic signs and instructions from airport operations personnel.
5. Drivers may not wait at active terminal curbs for future passenger requests.

## 4. Passenger Drop-off

1. Passenger drop-off is permitted at designated terminal departure areas.
2. Drivers must complete passenger drop-off promptly.
3. Drivers must not use pickup zones for passenger drop-off when airport operations has designated separate areas.
4. Vehicles must not remain stopped at terminal curbs after the passenger has completed the drop-off.

## 5. Driver Staging

1. Drivers waiting for airport requests must use the designated rideshare staging area.
2. Drivers must maintain their queue position while waiting for dispatch.
3. Drivers who voluntarily leave the staging area lose their queue position.
4. Drivers must not establish unofficial staging areas.
5. Airport operations may temporarily relocate staging during traffic or safety disruptions.

## 6. Airport Restrictions

Airport operations may temporarily restrict access to pickup areas during:

* Major traffic congestion
* Security incidents
* Terminal closures
* Road closures
* Emergency situations

Temporary restrictions must be communicated through the approved operations communication channel.

## 7. Approval Requirements

Airport Operations Manager approval is required for:

* Permanent changes to rideshare pickup zones.
* Permanent changes to staging locations.
* Operational exceptions lasting more than two hours.

Temporary safety restrictions may be implemented immediately by authorized airport operations personnel.

## 8. Compliance

Repeated violations of LAX rideshare operating procedures may result in operational review and temporary restrictions on airport dispatch activity.
