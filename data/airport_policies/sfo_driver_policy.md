# SFO Airport Driver Queue and Conduct Policy

**Policy ID:** SFO-DRV-001
**Airport:** San Francisco International Airport (SFO)
**Policy Type:** Driver Queue and Conduct
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

> **Synthetic Training Policy:** This document is fictional and created exclusively for the Airport Operations AI Copilot project.

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes driver queue, dispatch, and conduct requirements for SFO airport operations.

## 2. Queue Entry

1. Drivers must enter the designated rideshare staging area to become eligible for airport pickup requests.
2. Drivers must follow the established first-in, first-out queue.
3. Drivers must not create or join unofficial queues outside the designated staging area.

## 3. Queue Position

A driver must maintain their queue position while waiting for an eligible dispatch.

A driver who voluntarily leaves the designated staging area loses their current queue position.

The driver must re-enter the queue at the end of the available queue.

## 4. Dispatch

When a driver receives a valid airport dispatch, the driver may leave the staging area and proceed to the assigned pickup location.

Drivers must follow the designated route and airport instructions.

## 5. Abandoning the Queue

Drivers may not abandon the airport queue while retaining their queue position.

Leaving the staging area without an authorized dispatch or operational release results in loss of queue position.

## 6. Operational Release

Airport Operations may release drivers from the queue when:

* The staging area reaches operational capacity.
* A temporary airport restriction is activated.
* A safety issue requires relocation.
* Airport operations formally suspends the queue.

Drivers released by airport operations do not lose queue priority solely because of the operational release.

## 7. Driver Incentives

Any temporary airport-specific driver incentive must be approved through the applicable incentive approval process before activation.

## 8. Compliance

Repeated violations may result in review of the driver's airport operating privileges.
