# SFO Airport Pricing and Surge Policy

**Airport:** San Francisco International Airport (SFO)

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines pricing and surge requirements for rideshare services operating at SFO.

## 2. Standard Pricing

Standard trip pricing must follow the applicable platform pricing configuration.

Operations teams may monitor pricing conditions and demand levels without additional approval.

## 3. Surge Pricing

Surge pricing may be used when airport demand materially exceeds available driver supply.

The maximum standard surge multiplier permitted at SFO without additional approval is **1.5x**.

## 4. Surge Above 1.5x

Any increase above **1.5x** requires approval from the **Airport Operations Manager** before implementation.

The request must include:

* Current demand conditions
* Available driver supply
* Estimated supply-demand imbalance
* Reason for the proposed increase
* Expected duration

## 5. Emergency Conditions

During a verified airport disruption or emergency, a temporary surge increase above 1.5x may be proposed.

However, the increase still requires Airport Operations Manager approval before activation unless an emergency operating procedure explicitly authorizes immediate action.

## 6. Duration

Approved surge increases must specify an expected duration.

Operations teams should review active surge conditions at least every 30 minutes during a disruption.

## 7. Monitoring

Operations teams must monitor:

* Active driver supply
* Passenger demand
* Pickup wait times
* Queue length
* Trip cancellation rates

If conditions return to normal, surge pricing should be reduced according to the approved operating plan.

## 8. Compliance

Unapproved surge increases above 1.5x are not permitted under this policy.
