# SFO Airport Operations Policy

**Policy ID:** SFO-OPS-001
**Airport:** San Francisco International Airport (SFO)
**Policy Type:** Airport Operations
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

> **Synthetic Training Policy:** This document is fictional and created exclusively for the Airport Operations AI Copilot project.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines operational rules for rideshare driver activity, passenger pickup, passenger drop-off, and vehicle staging at San Francisco International Airport (SFO).

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to rideshare drivers operating within designated SFO airport areas.

## 3. Pickup Rules

1. Drivers must use the designated rideshare pickup area assigned by airport operations.
2. Drivers may not collect passengers from unauthorized terminal curb locations.
3. Drivers must follow airport signage and instructions from airport operations personnel.
4. Drivers may proceed to the pickup area only after receiving a valid passenger dispatch.
5. Drivers must not wait for passenger requests at active terminal curbs.

## 4. Drop-off Rules

1. Passenger drop-off is permitted at designated terminal departure areas.
2. Drivers must follow posted curbside restrictions.
3. Drivers must not remain stopped at the terminal curb longer than necessary to complete passenger drop-off.
4. Drivers must not use passenger pickup areas for extended vehicle staging.

## 5. Queue and Staging

1. Drivers waiting for airport pickup requests must use the designated rideshare staging area.
2. Drivers must enter the queue in an orderly manner and maintain their position.
3. Drivers must not create secondary queues outside the designated staging area.
4. Drivers who leave the designated staging area voluntarily lose their queue position.
5. Drivers may leave the staging area after receiving a dispatch or when explicitly released by airport operations.

## 6. Operational Disruptions

During significant operational disruptions, airport operations may temporarily modify pickup or staging procedures.

Temporary operational changes must be communicated through the approved operations communication channel.

## 7. Approval Requirements

The following actions require Airport Operations Manager approval:

* Permanent changes to designated pickup areas.
* Changes to airport staging locations.
* Exceptions to standard airport operating procedures lasting more than two hours.

Temporary safety-related instructions issued by airport operations personnel may take immediate effect and do not require prior approval.

## 8. Compliance

Drivers who repeatedly violate airport pickup, drop-off, or staging requirements may be subject to operational review and temporary restriction from airport dispatch activity.
