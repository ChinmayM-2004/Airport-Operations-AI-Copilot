## 1. Purpose

This policy defines pricing and surge management requirements for rideshare services operating at John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK).

## 2. Standard Pricing

Standard trip pricing must follow the approved platform pricing configuration.

Operations teams may monitor pricing, passenger demand, and driver supply without additional approval.

## 3. Surge Pricing

Surge pricing may be activated when passenger demand materially exceeds available driver supply.

The maximum surge multiplier permitted at JFK without additional approval is **2.0x**.

## 4. Surge Above 2.0x

Any proposed surge increase above **2.0x** requires approval from the **Airport Operations Manager** before activation.

The approval request must include:

* Current passenger demand
* Available driver supply
* Average passenger wait time
* Current cancellation rate
* Estimated supply-demand imbalance
* Reason for the proposed increase
* Expected duration

## 5. Operational Disruptions

During major airport disruptions, operations teams may propose temporary pricing adjustments.

Examples include:

* Large flight arrival waves
* Terminal closures
* Road closures
* Significant traffic incidents
* Security restrictions
* Major weather-related disruptions

Any surge above 2.0x remains subject to Airport Operations Manager approval unless a separately approved emergency procedure explicitly authorizes immediate action.

## 6. Monitoring

During elevated surge conditions, operations teams should monitor:

* Passenger demand
* Available driver supply
* Queue length
* Passenger wait time
* Cancellation rate

Pricing conditions should be reassessed at least every 30 minutes during an active disruption.

## 7. Surge Reduction

When passenger demand and driver supply return to normal operating conditions, surge should be reduced according to the approved operating plan.

## 8. Approval Documentation

Approved surge increases must document:

* Approved multiplier
* Approving manager
* Reason for increase
* Start time
* Expected end time
* Operational conditions supporting the decision

## 9. Compliance

Surge above **2.0x** must not be activated without the required approval.
