# JFK Airport Operations Policy

**Policy ID:** JFK-OPS-001
**Airport:** John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK)
**Policy Type:** Airport Operations
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01

> **Synthetic Training Policy:** This document is fictional and created exclusively for the Airport Operations AI Copilot project.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines operational requirements for rideshare pickup, passenger drop-off, driver staging, and airport access at John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK).

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to rideshare drivers and airport operations teams managing rideshare activity at JFK.

## 3. Passenger Pickup

1. Drivers must use the designated rideshare pickup areas assigned by airport operations.
2. Drivers must not collect passengers from unauthorized terminal curb locations.
3. Drivers may proceed to a pickup area only after receiving a valid passenger dispatch.
4. Drivers must follow airport traffic signs and instructions from airport operations personnel.
5. Drivers must not wait at active terminal curbs for future passenger requests.

## 4. Passenger Drop-off

1. Passenger drop-off is permitted at designated terminal departure areas.
2. Drivers must follow posted curbside restrictions.
3. Drivers must complete passenger drop-off promptly.
4. Drivers must not use designated pickup areas for extended drop-off activity.
5. Vehicles must leave the terminal curb after completing passenger drop-off.

## 5. Driver Staging and Queue

1. Drivers waiting for airport pickup requests must use the designated rideshare staging area.
2. Drivers must follow the established queue order.
3. Drivers must maintain their queue position while waiting for dispatch.
4. Drivers who voluntarily leave the staging area lose their current queue position.
5. Drivers must not establish unofficial staging areas outside the designated facility.

## 6. Airport Access Restrictions

Airport operations may temporarily restrict rideshare access during:

* Terminal congestion
* Security incidents
* Road closures
* Terminal closures
* Emergency situations

Temporary restrictions must be communicated through the approved operations communication channel.

## 7. Operational Disruptions

During significant disruptions, airport operations may temporarily modify pickup, drop-off, or staging procedures.

Authorized airport operations personnel may issue temporary safety instructions that take immediate effect.

## 8. Approval Requirements

Airport Operations Manager approval is required for:

* Permanent changes to designated pickup areas.
* Permanent changes to staging locations.
* Operational exceptions lasting more than two hours.

Temporary safety-related restrictions may be implemented immediately by authorized airport operations personnel.

## 9. Compliance

Repeated violations of JFK airport operating procedures may result in operational review and temporary restrictions on airport dispatch activity.
